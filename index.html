Soil Color Pro Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy covers Soil Color Pro for iPhone, iPad, and Android. Soil Color Pro does not collect personal data, does not create an account, and does not upload photos, camera frames, readings, or locations. It has no server.

Camera

Used only to show a live preview and to estimate color on the device.

Photos

Photos are read only when you choose one in the system photo picker. Analysis stays on this device.

Location

Used only when you save a pin. Coordinates stay on this device. They appear in CSV, Excel, or Google Earth files only if you export them.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple on iPhone and iPad, and by Google Play on Android, under their privacy policies. Soil Color Pro does not receive your payment card details. On Android, the app's only network traffic is Google Play's purchase service.

Contact

If you email plancktime@protonmail.com, that email is used only to reply.
