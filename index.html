Soil Color Pro Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Soil Color Pro does not collect personal data, does not create an account, and does not upload photos or camera frames.

Camera

Used only to show a live preview and to estimate color on the device.

Photos

Photo library access is used only when you choose an existing image. Analysis stays on this device.

Location

Used only when you save a pin. Coordinates stay on this device. They appear in Excel or Google Earth files only if you export them.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. Soil Color Pro does not receive your payment card details.

Contact

If you email plancktime@protonmail.com, that email is used only to reply.
