Soil Color Pro Support

Soil Color Pro names soil color from the camera on your iPhone, iPad, or Android phone. Analysis stays on the device.

Live naming is free

Point Live at soil to see the most likely name and other likely chips. One five-minute Live session is free; after it ends, the Pro options open automatically. Continued Live use, gray card and white paper calibration on photos, Capture, photo tools, pins, CSV, Excel, and Google Earth KMZ export require Pro. Pro is available monthly, yearly, or as a lifetime unlock. Eligible new yearly subscribers can choose a 7-day free trial, then the plan renews at the price shown before purchase unless canceled.

Calibrating with a gray card or white paper

Live readings are uncalibrated estimates. To calibrate, capture or open a photo with the card in the same light as the soil, choose the reference in scan setup, and drag the Reference marker onto the card. Nothing is detected automatically, so a gray table or white wall is never mistaken for your card. Until the marker is placed, the reading stays uncalibrated.

Restore a purchase

Open Field and tap Restore purchases. To manage a subscription on iPhone or iPad, use Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. On Android, use Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
